Common questions

Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

No sound when recording: allow Microphone for Helivo in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Captions missing: captions need on-device speech recognition for your language (iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Dictation).

Helivo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Helivo: Voice Changer Video (“Helivo”, “we”) changes your voice with sound effects and films vertical videos. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your voice and videos stay on your iPhone

Recording, voice effects, video export and captions all run on your device. Your recordings are stored in the app's private storage until you delete them or the app. Nothing you record is uploaded to us or to anyone else. Videos are added to Photos only when you tap Save; Share sends a file only where you choose.

Captions

Captions use Apple's on-device speech recognition. Helivo asks iOS to keep recognition on the device; if on-device recognition is not available for your language, the app does not make captions.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Adapty to manage subscriptions and to measure how the app is used (for example, which onboarding step was reached, which plan was chosen, and answers such as what you want to make). Adapty receives a random profile identifier, device and purchase information and your IP-derived country. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or location, and we do not track you across other companies' apps.

Children

Helivo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

Delete takes in the app, or delete the app to remove all local data. For questions or data requests, email us.

Processors

Apple (App Store purchases, on-device speech recognition), Adapty (subscriptions and analytics).
